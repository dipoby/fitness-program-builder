# 8-Week Beginner Full-Body Fat-Loss Program (Bodyweight + Dumbbell)

*Approved. Run: `run-001-beginner-home-fatloss`, final_draft version 1,
approved 2026-01-15.*

## Summary

A 3-day/week, 8-week full-body program for a true beginner training at home
with just dumbbells and bodyweight, designed to support fat loss while
preserving muscle through moderate-rep resistance training. Includes a
week-6 deload.

## Progression Plan

**Strategy:** Double progression within each exercise's assigned rep range —
build up reps at a given load/variant before increasing difficulty. RPE
guidance ramps from weeks 1-2 through weeks 7-8, with a planned deload in
week 6.

| Weeks | Intensity | What changes |
|---|---|---|
| 1-2 | RPE 6-7 | Learn the movements; 3-4 reps left in reserve every set. |
| 3-5 | RPE 7-8 | Push toward the top of each rep range with control. If you hit the top of the range on all sets for 2 sessions running, increase load ~2.5-5% (dumbbell lifts) or move to a harder bodyweight variant. |
| 6 (deload) | Reduced | One fewer set per exercise, same rep range, intentionally easier — this is recovery, not a setback. |
| 7-8 | RPE 8 | Resume progressing; top of the rep range should feel genuinely challenging but technically clean. |

*(Full week-by-week, exercise-by-exercise detail is in `artifacts/progression_plan.json`.)*

## Safety Notes

- No injuries were confirmed, but all exercises are low technical-complexity
  and appropriate for a true beginner.
- Stop a set short of the target reps if form breaks down; form takes
  priority over hitting a number.
- Deload in week 6 is intentional — resist the urge to push through it at
  full intensity.
