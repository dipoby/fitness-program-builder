# 8-Week Beginner Fat-Loss Full-Body Program (Dumbbell and Bodyweight, Knee-Friendly)

## Summary

An 8-week, 3-day-per-week full-body program (Tuesday/Thursday/Saturday, about 35-40 minutes per session) for a beginner whose goal is fat loss, using only adjustable dumbbells (about 2-20 kg per hand) and bodyweight at home. All knee-dominant work is kept shallow-to-moderate because of pain with deep knee bending. Week 1 is a ramp-in at 2 sets per exercise, weeks 2-7 build volume and effort using double progression, and week 8 is a lighter deload. An optional easier week is available if you feel unusually fatigued around weeks 4-5.

## Confirmed Requirements

- Goal: fat loss
- Experience: beginner
- Equipment: dumbbell (about 2-20 kg per hand), bodyweight
- Days per week: 3 (Tuesday/Thursday/Saturday)
- Session length: 40 minutes
- Constraint (injury): Pain with deep knee bending; avoid deep squats/lunges and deep knee flexion under load. Keep knee range of motion shallow-to-moderate. No other injuries.
- Notes: Home training. Adjustable dumbbells approx. 2-20 kg per hand. Schedule: Tuesday/Thursday/Saturday.

## Progression Plan

Double progression within each exercise's rep range (per research.json finding for beginner fat-loss training: moderate rep ranges, 1-3 RIR, dumbbell loads capped around 20 kg). Week 1 starts one set lighter on 3-set exercises, weeks 2-7 run the prescribed sets while RIR tightens from 3 to 1-2, and week 8 is a lighter consolidation/deload. Once rep-range tops are reached, add load in small steps, or, when dumbbells cap out, add tempo, pauses, or shorter rest. Knee flexion stays shallow-to-moderate throughout. If you feel unusually fatigued or sore around weeks 4-5, take an easier week (drop to 2 sets per exercise at about 3 RIR) before continuing.

- Week 1 (ramp-in): 2 sets per exercise, about 3 reps in reserve (RIR); stay at the lower end of each rep range.
- Weeks 2-7: prescribed sets as listed above.
- Week 8 (deload): 2 sets per exercise, about 4 RIR, about 10-20% less load or an easier pace.
- Optional easier week (around weeks 4-5, only if unusually fatigued): 2 sets per exercise at about 3 RIR, then continue.

| Week | Effort (RIR) | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | 3 RIR (about 3 reps left) | Ramp-in, 2 sets per exercise |
| 2 | 3 RIR (about 3 reps left) | Prescribed sets |
| 3 | 2-3 RIR | Prescribed sets |
| 4 | 2 RIR | Prescribed sets |
| 5 | 2 RIR | Prescribed sets |
| 6 | 1-2 RIR | Prescribed sets |
| 7 | 1-2 RIR (hardest week, never to failure) | Prescribed sets |
| 8 | 4 RIR (easy, about 60-70% of normal effort) | Deload |

Load rule (double progression): once you hit the top of the rep range on all sets for 2 consecutive sessions with good form, increase load about 2.5-5% (smallest jump available). Dumbbells cap at about 20 kg, so when the cap is reached add reps, slow the 3-sec lowering, pause, or shorten rest. Bodyweight moves progress by reps, then tempo or pauses. Timed holds: add about 5 seconds per set.

## Equipment & Substitutions

- Required: adjustable dumbbells (about 2-20 kg per hand) and bodyweight only.
- Pull exercise: Dumbbell Bent-Over Row is the only dumbbell/bodyweight pull option available. Bench rows (single-arm row, chest-supported row) are excluded because no bench is available, and the exercise database returns no bench-free substitutes. If a bench becomes available, swap db_single_arm_row into Day 2.
- Reverse Lunge was returned by the catalogue but deliberately not used (knee precaution).
- If dumbbell load caps out, progress with reps, tempo, pauses, or shorter rest.

## Safety Notes

- Knee precaution: you have pain with deep knee bending. Keep knee bend shallow-to-moderate (pain-free, box-limited), and do not do deep squats, deep lunges, or loaded deep knee flexion. No loaded squats or lunges are programmed. Stop any movement that reproduces knee pain.
- Bodyweight Squat: limit depth to a pain-free shallow-to-moderate range (box or well above parallel); stop if knee pain recurs.
- Dumbbell Bent-Over Row and Romanian Deadlift: neutral spine, hinge at the hips; reduce load if your lower back fatigues.
- Pull exercises: Dumbbell Bent-Over Row (db_row) is the only available dumbbell/bodyweight pull exercise suitable for a beginner. Bench-based rows (single-arm row, chest-supported row) are excluded because you have no bench; Renegade Row is advanced and loads the wrists. db_row is therefore used in all three sessions, varied by rep range and tempo.
- Week 1 is a ramp-in (2 sets per exercise, about 3 reps in reserve). Week 8 is a deload (about 4 reps in reserve, lighter load or easier pace). Never train to true failure as a beginner.
- Optional easier week: if you feel unusually fatigued or sore around weeks 4-5, drop to 2 sets per exercise at about 3 reps in reserve for one week, then continue.
- If knee pain persists, see a clinician. The research sources are general-audience and secondary, and this program is not clinical or medical advice.
- Nutrition is the main driver of fat loss and is outside the scope of this program. Optional knee-friendly activity on off days: walking, cycling, or swimming.
