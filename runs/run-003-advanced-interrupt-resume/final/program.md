# 12-Week Advanced Muscle-Gain Program (Kettlebell + Band + Pull-Up Bar)

*Approved. Run: `run-003-advanced-interrupt-resume`, final_draft version 2, approved 2026-03-18.*

## Summary

A 5-day/week, 12-week push/pull/legs + upper/lower program for an advanced
trainee working with only a kettlebell, resistance bands, a pull-up bar, and
bodyweight. Progresses through reps, tempo, and reduced rest since equipment
limits simple load jumps, with a week-9 deload.

## Progression Plan

**Strategy:** Volume- and variation-based progression — since simple load
jumps are limited by kettlebell/band/bodyweight-only equipment, progress via
added reps first, then tempo (slower eccentrics), then reduced rest for
metabolic stress, with an extra top-up set added in the final push (weeks
10-12) after the week-9 deload.

| Weeks | Intensity | What changes |
|---|---|---|
| 1-4 | RPE 7-8 | Build baseline volume tolerance across all 5 sessions. |
| 5-8 | RPE 8 | Add a slower eccentric tempo (3-4s lowering) once comfortable at the top of the rep range. |
| 9 (deload) | Reduced | One fewer set per exercise, same or lighter effort. |
| 10-12 | RPE 8-9 | Add a top-up set per exercise; combine top-of-range reps, tempo, and ~15s less rest. |

*(Full week-by-week, exercise-by-exercise detail is in `artifacts/progression_plan.json`.)*

## Equipment & Substitutions

Equipment used: **bodyweight, kettlebell, resistance-band, pull-up-bar** —
matches what you confirmed. Day 4 deliberately uses different push/pull
variations (incline push-up, band row) than Days 1/2, to add variety given
the limited equipment pool — no substitutions were otherwise needed.

## Safety Notes

- No injuries were confirmed; exercise selection still favors controllable,
  joint-friendly movements appropriate for unsupervised training.
- **Before Kettlebell Swings specifically (Day 3):** spend 1-2 minutes on hip
  hinge/glute activation (e.g. a few slow bodyweight glute bridges) before
  swinging — swinging cold is a common way to tweak the lower back.
- The week-9 deload is intentional — resist the urge to push through it at
  full volume.
